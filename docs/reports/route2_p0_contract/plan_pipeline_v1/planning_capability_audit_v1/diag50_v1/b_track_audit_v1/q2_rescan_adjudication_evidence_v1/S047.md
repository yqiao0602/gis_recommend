## Instruction A（全文）

Task: Estimate start and end of season
Estimate the start (SoS) and end (EoS) of the growing season using a threshold-based method on a vegetation index (e.g., NDVI) derived from Sentinel-2 surface reflectance data. It computes the seasonal amplitude, applies a threshold (percentage of amplitude above minimum VI), identifies the first and last dates when the vegetation index crosses this threshold, and performs linear interpolation between adjacent observations to refine SoS and EoS in day of year (DOY). Outputs include interpolated SoS and EoS raster maps, a time series chart at a point of interest with SoS/EoS markers, and interactive legends for visualization.

## Instruction B（全文）

Task: Estimate start and end of season
Estimate the start of season (SoS) and end of season (EoS) using a threshold-based method on a vegetation index (e.g., EVI, NDVI) derived from Sentinel-2 surface reflectance data. It computes the seasonal amplitude, applies a threshold relative to the minimum and maximum values, identifies the first and last dates when the vegetation index crosses this threshold, and performs linear interpolation to refine SoS and EoS in day of year. Outputs include interpolated SoS and EoS raster maps, a time series chart with SoS/EoS markers at a point of interest, and corresponding legends displayed on the map.
