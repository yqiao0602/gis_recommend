## Instruction A（全文）

Task: Analyze urban sprawl around protected areas
Analyze urban expansion around protected areas using nighttime light data from VIIRS and DMSP-OLS sensors between 1999 and 2019. It computes smoothed annual mean nighttime lights, calculates differences across decades (1999–2009, 2009–2019), applies Gaussian convolution for spatial smoothing, and classifies urban growth intensity into zones using thresholding. The output includes classified raster layers for urban sprawl visualization, buffer zones (10km, 15km, 20km) around protected areas, administrative boundaries, and tabular summaries of lit area (in m²) within buffers for three time points (1999, 2009, 2019), exported as CSV files. A legend is added for visual interpretation of urban intensity levels.

## Instruction B（全文）

Task: Analyze urban sprawl around protected areas
Analyze the growth of human habitation around protected areas using nighttime light data from VIIRS and DMSP sensors between 1999 and 2019. It computes smoothed annual mean nighttime lights, applies Gaussian convolution for spatial smoothing, calculates differences in nighttime light intensity across decades (1999–2009 and 2009–2019), and classifies urban sprawl into zones based on thresholds. The output includes classified raster layers for urban expansion (1999–2009 and 2009–2019), buffer zones (10km, 15km, 20km) around protected areas, state boundary visualization, and tabular summaries of lit area (in m²) within buffers for three time points (1999, 2009, 2019), exported as CSV files to Google Drive. A legend and map labels are added for visualization.
