## Instruction A（全文）

Task: Analyze ET and precipitation correlation
Compute the Spearman's rank correlation between monthly evapotranspiration (ET) from MODIS MOD16A2 and monthly precipitation from CHIRPS over a defined region. It first aggregates daily CHIRPS and 8-day MOD16A2 data into monthly sums, resamples CHIRPS to match MODIS resolution using bilinear interpolation, joins the collections by timestamp, combines them into a single image collection, and calculates pixel-wise Spearman's correlation across time. The output is a raster map of correlation coefficients (-1 to 1) which is clipped to a user-defined region and exported to Google Drive.

## Instruction B（全文）

Task: Analyze ET precipitation correlation
Compute the Spearman's rank correlation between monthly evapotranspiration (ET) from MODIS and monthly precipitation from CHIRPS over a defined time period. It first aggregates daily CHIRPS precipitation and 8-day MOD16A2 ET data into monthly sums, resamples CHIRPS to match MODIS resolution using bilinear interpolation, joins the collections by timestamp, combines them into a single image collection, and calculates pixel-wise non-parametric correlation across time. The output is a raster map of correlation coefficients ranging from -1 to 1, which is clipped to a user-defined region and exported to Google Drive.
