## Instruction A（全文）

Task: Analyze reservoir eutrophication trends
Analyze long-term changes in water body area, chlorophyll-a concentration, and eutrophication percentage for a specific point location using multi-sensor satellite data from 1987 to 2021. It computes NDWI for water extraction, estimates chlorophyll-a via a band ratio model, and calculates the proportion of eutrophic areas over time, generating time-series charts for area, Chl-a, and eutrophication percentage.
