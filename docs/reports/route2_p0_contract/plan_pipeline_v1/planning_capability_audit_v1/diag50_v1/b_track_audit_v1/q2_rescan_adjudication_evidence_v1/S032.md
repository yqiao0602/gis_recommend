## Instruction A（全文）

Task: Analyze backscatter variability in land cover types
Analyze temporal backscatter variability of Sentinel-1 SAR data over four land cover types (crop, forest, urban, crop2) within a study area. It converts VV band backscatter from decibel to power domain, computes monthly maximum composites from January 2017 to December 2018, and derives statistical metrics including mean, standard deviation, and coefficient of variation. The outputs include a coefficient of variation raster map, a binarized classification based on a 0.4 threshold, time series scatter charts of monthly backscatter by region, and a bar chart comparing coefficient of variance across regions.
