Estimate chlorophyll-a concentration in inland water bodies using Sentinel-2 data by computing the NDCI (Normalized Difference Chlorophyll Index) from atmospherically corrected surface reflectance, applying a semi-empirical algorithm (Lobo et al.), and classifying water quality based on NDCI thresholds. ## Instruction B（全文）

Task: Estimate chlorophyll-a concentration
Estimate chlorophyll-a concentration in surface water using Sentinel-2 data by computing the NDCI (Normalized Difference Chlorophyll Index) from atmospherically corrected reflectance bands B4 and B5, applying a semi-empirical model (Lobo et al.), and classifying water quality based on NDCI thresholds. Daily median composites are generated for each day in the period and exported as an ImageCollection asset.
