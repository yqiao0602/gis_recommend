## 任务与步骤要求

**Task PCA2-T-070 全文**：

Task: Estimate vegetation cover fraction
Estimate fractional vegetation cover (FVC) from the greenest pixel composite of Landsat 5 surface reflectance data using NDVI, applying percentile-based minimum and maximum NDVI values within a region of interest. It classifies FVC into five coverage levels, computes area statistics per class, and exports classified FVC and NDVI maps to Google Drive.

**Step 1**：Data Preparation & ROI Definition ｜ purpose: Isolate the study area from the full scene ｜ input: Raw Landsat 5 SR composite ｜ output: Cropped image stack aligned to ROI
