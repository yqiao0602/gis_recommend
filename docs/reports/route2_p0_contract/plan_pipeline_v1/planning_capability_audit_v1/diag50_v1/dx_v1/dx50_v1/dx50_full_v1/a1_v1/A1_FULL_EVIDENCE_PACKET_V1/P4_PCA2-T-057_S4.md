## 任务与步骤要求

**Task PCA2-T-057 全文**：

Task: Analyze Sentinel-1 speckle reduction
the workflow evaluates speckle noise reduction in Sentinel-1 SAR imagery over a defined polygon using two filtering methods: focal median smoothing and an edge-preserving directional smoothing (EDS) algorithm. It computes the mean squared difference (MSD) between filtered images and the original to assess performance, outputs include visualizations of raw, smoothed, and EDS-filtered images on the map, along with printed MSD values for quantitative comparison.

**Step 4**：Compute Mean Squared Differences ｜ purpose: Quantitatively measure the pixel-wise deviation of each filtered version from the original ｜ input: Original image (from Step 1), Focal Median result (Step 2), EDS result (Step 3) ｜ output: Two scalar values (Focal Median MSD, EDS MSD).
