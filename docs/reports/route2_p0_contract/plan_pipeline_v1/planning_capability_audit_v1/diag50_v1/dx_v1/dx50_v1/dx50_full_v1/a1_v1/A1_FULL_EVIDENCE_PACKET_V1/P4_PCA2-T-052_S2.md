**Task PCA2-T-052 全文**：

Task: Analyze surface reflectance spectra
the workflow selects the least cloudy Landsat-8 OLI/TIRS Collection 2 Level-2 surface reflectance image over a region of interest (roi) from January to May 2019. It extracts spectral bands SR_B1 to SR_B7 and computes mean reflectance values for user-defined feature classes (Water, Forest, City) using point samples. Two scatter charts are generated: one with default band indexing and another mapped to actual wavelengths (443–2201 nm), visualizing the spectral signatures of land cover types. Outputs include a false-color image layer on the map and two interactive charts showing reflectance profiles across wavelengths.
