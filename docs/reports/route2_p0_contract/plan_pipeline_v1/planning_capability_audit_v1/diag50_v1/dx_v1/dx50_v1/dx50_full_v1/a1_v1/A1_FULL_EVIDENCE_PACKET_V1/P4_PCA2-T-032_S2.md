## 任务与步骤要求

**Task PCA2-T-032 全文**：

Task: Estimate reservoir water extent
Calculate the daily water surface area of Massingir Reservoir using Landsat-derived NDWI (Normalized Difference Water Index) time series from 1984 to 2020. It mosaics and filters Landsat 4–8 images by date and cloud cover, computes NDWI using green and NIR bands, applies a threshold (0.1) to classify water, masks non-water pixels, and estimates the water area in km² per day via pixel area summation. The output is a time-series chart of water extent over time and median composites for visualization.

**Step 2**：Radiometric Calibration & Atmospheric Correction ｜ purpose: Convert digital numbers to top-of-atmosphere reflectance and apply an atmospheric correction model to minimize scattering effects. ｜ input: Step 1 output ｜ output: Atmospherically corrected reflectance datasets ready for index calculation.
