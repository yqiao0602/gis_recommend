## 任务与步骤要求

**Task PCA2-T-060 全文**：

Task: Estimate GEDI lidar metrics by tile
the workflow trains and tests Random Forest regression models to estimate 13 non-FHD (non-forest height distribution) GEDI-derived LiDAR metrics—including canopy height percentiles (RHD_25to50, RHD_50to75, etc.), rh98, cover, pai, and vertical PAVD layers (0–10m to >60m)—for each 60-km tile across Eastern North America. For each response variable and tile subset (50 tiles per batch), it: (1) splits vectorized ground-truth samples (n=26.5M) into training (80%) and testing sets using tile-specific random seeds; (2) trains an RF classifier with tuned hyperparameters (variablesPerSplit, minLeafPopulation, bagFraction) extracted from a prior hyperparameter tuning round (Round_ID=3, Tuning_ID=2); (3) computes RMSE and R² on held-out test samples; (4) applies the model to a stacked 93-band environmental predictor image (including HLSL30, Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, ALOS topography, ESRI/GLC land cover, leaf traits, and soil properties) to generate per-tile continuous surface estimates; (5) ranks and exports the top 20 most important predictors per model; and (6) exports both the tiled estimation rasters (as multi-band ImageAssets) and per-tile accuracy/variable-importance FeatureCollection assets.

**Step 1**：Sample Preparation & Splitting ｜ purpose: Organize ground-truth samples into tile-based training/testing subsets ｜ input: Raw vectorized GEDI samples ｜ output: Tile-indexed training and testing sample arrays
