**Task PCA2-T-025 全文**：

Task: Compute Sentinel-1 VH backscatter mean
Compute the temporal mean of Sentinel-1 C-band VH polarized radar backscatter over a two-month ascending-orbit window (2021-09-25 to 2021-11-25) to achieve temporal despeckling. It filters the COPERNICUS/S1_GRD collection for IW mode, VH polarization, and ascending orbits only; selects the 'VH' band; applies a date filter; computes the pixel-wise mean across all qualifying images; and visualizes the resulting single-band backscatter raster with a linear stretch (min=−25 dB, max=5 dB) centered on Scotland. The output is a cloud-free (radar-unaffected), speckle-reduced, georeferenced raster layer representing average surface microwave reflectivity at C-band VH polarization.

**Step 5**：Visual Enhancement ｜ purpose: Apply a linear stretch to the mean raster to optimize contrast for visualization, mapping −25 dB to black and 5 dB to white ｜ input: Mean backscatter raster ｜ output: Final raster with enhanced visual appearance
