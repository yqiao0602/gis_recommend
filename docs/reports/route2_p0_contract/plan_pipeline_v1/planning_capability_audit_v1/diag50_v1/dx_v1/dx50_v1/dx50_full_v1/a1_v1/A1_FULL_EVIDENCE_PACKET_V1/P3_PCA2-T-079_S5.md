## 任务与步骤要求

**Task PCA2-T-079 全文**：

Task: Extract urban LST near stations
Extract nighttime land surface temperature (LST) values from MODIS for Beijing, Guangzhou, and Shanghai within variable-radius buffers around meteorological stations. It computes monthly mean LST for 2015, buffers the stations from 50 to 300 meters in 50-meter increments, and uses reduceRegions to extract mean LST values per buffer. Outputs include a FeatureCollection of buffered station regions with mean LST per month and radius, and visualizations of the composite LST image and specific station locations.

**Step 5**：Produce Visualizations ｜ purpose: Create summary maps for interpretation and presentation ｜ input: Final enriched FeatureCollection and/or processed LST composites ｜ output: Two map images: one displaying the full-year composite LST, another highlighting the station locations and their corresponding buffer zones.
