## 任务与步骤要求

**Task PCA2-T-062 全文**：

Task: Analyze northern spotted owl habitat suitability and spatial patterns
Visualize and compares annual maps (1986–2022) of northern spotted owl (NSO) habitat suitability classes and spatial patterns (core, edge, finger, scatter) across the Northwest Forest Plan (NWFP) region. It enables interactive selection of physiographic provinces, land ownership, federal reserves, agencies, units, Late Successional Reserves (LSRs), and Critical Habitat subunits to generate trend charts of habitat change over time based on user-defined areas of interest.

**Step 4**：Visualization Framework Development ｜ purpose: Build the foundational interface for map viewing, including controls for panning, zooming, and toggling between different years' maps. Establish the canvas where filtered views will appear. ｜ input: Output from Step 3 ｜ output: Interactive map viewer component capable of loading and displaying any single annual map.
