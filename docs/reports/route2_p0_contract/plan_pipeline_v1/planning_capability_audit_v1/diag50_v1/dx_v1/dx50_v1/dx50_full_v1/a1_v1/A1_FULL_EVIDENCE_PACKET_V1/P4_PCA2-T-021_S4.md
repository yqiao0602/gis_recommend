## 任务与步骤要求

**Task PCA2-T-021 全文**：

Task: Extract river width from S1/S2 time series
Compute river width over a defined AOI using Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 time series imagery from 2015 to 2019. It preprocesses SAR data (VV/VH) with speckle filtering, terrain flattening, and border noise correction, and fuses water masks derived from both sensors using multiple methods (e.g., Zou2018). Water masks are constrained by JRC max water extent, and widths are extracted along GRWL centerlines within 4000 m distance, removing spurious branches shorter than 150 m. The final output is a time-series collection of mean river widths per image, exported as a CSV table.

**Step 4**：Fuse Multi-Sensor Water Masks ｜ purpose: Combine strengths of different sensors to create a robust, single-source-of-truth water presence map ｜ input: Step 3 individual sensor masks ｜ output: One primary fused_water_mask_raster for each acquisition date.
