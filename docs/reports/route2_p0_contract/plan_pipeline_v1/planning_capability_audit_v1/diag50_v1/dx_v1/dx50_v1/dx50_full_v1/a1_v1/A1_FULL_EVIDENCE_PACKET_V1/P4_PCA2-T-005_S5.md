**Task PCA2-T-005 全文**：

Task: Analyze fire-affected area using Landsat imagery
Analyze the SLATER wildfire by extracting its spatial bounds and discovery date, then filters Landsat 8 surface reflectance images from the start date to three months after with low cloud cover. It visualizes true-color and false-color composites of the selected image to assess burn extent and computes pixel value ranges (min/max) across the image bounds for spectral analysis.

**Step 5**：Burn Area Mask Creation ｜ purpose: To define the analytical region of interest for quantitative assessment ｜ input: Output from Step 1 ｜ output: Binary raster mask where pixels inside the fire boundary are set to 1 and outside to 0.
