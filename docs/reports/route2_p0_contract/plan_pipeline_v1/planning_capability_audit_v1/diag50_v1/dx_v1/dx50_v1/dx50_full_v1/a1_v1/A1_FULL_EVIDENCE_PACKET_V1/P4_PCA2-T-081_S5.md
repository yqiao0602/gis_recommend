## 任务与步骤要求

**Task PCA2-T-081 全文**：

Task: Identify sugarcane cultivation area
Identify sugarcane cultivation areas in a specific region using EVI and NDVI time series derived from Sentinel-2 or Landsat imagery (implied by vegetation indices) from 2020 to 2021. It computes phenological metrics including green-up date (GUD), seasonally detrended peak stage (SDPS), senescence date (SD), growth rate (GUS), and length of season (LOS). A multi-criteria thresholding approach is applied based on these metrics, combined with elevation constraints from SRTM, to generate a final sugarcane distribution map. The output includes a classified raster map of sugarcane extent and its total area calculated within the region of interest.

**Step 5**：Topographic Constraint Integration ｜ purpose: Refine mask using elevation suitability rules ｜ input: Step 4 mask, SRTM DEM ｜ output: Final refined sugarcane distribution raster
