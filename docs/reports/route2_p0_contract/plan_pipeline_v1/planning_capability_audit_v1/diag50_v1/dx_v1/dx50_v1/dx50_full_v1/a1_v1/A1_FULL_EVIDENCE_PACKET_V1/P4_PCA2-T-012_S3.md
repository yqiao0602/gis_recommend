## 任务与步骤要求

**Task PCA2-T-012 全文**：

Task: Analyze rice cultivation EVI anomaly
Analyze the cumulative Enhanced Vegetation Index (EVI) anomaly in rice cultivation areas on the Salween River's east side within Myanmar, specifically focusing on the Alaungdaw Kathapa National Park region. It uses MODIS MOD13Q1 EVI data from 2019–2020 as a baseline to compute anomalies for 2021–2022. The workflow involves filtering the study area by land use name, calculating mean baseline EVI, mapping anomaly values over the recent period, accumulating these anomalies over time, and generating a time series chart of cumulative EVI deviation. Outputs include a cumulative anomaly map, a regional time series chart of ecosystem vegetation change, and export of the clipped anomaly raster to Google Drive.

**Step 3**：Recent Data Processing ｜ purpose: Prepare the current period data for comparison ｜ input: Clipped MODIS EVI data for 2021-2022 ｜ output: Raster grid containing the mean EVI value per pixel for the recent period.
